Beyond the Silence: Breaking the Stigma Surrounding Mental Health

ASTRA INK. · EDITORIAL

A person’s mental well-being is never a measure of their character or strength, and it should be discussed without fear or judgment. It should not be considered a privilege reserved only for people who can afford support and have enough financial resources. Mental health is a fundamental part of human life, and yet we continue to turn a blind eye. Nowadays, society continues to ignore it and fails to provide enough understanding and support. Behind every ordinary smile, cheerful personality, and daily conversation are individuals who face mental struggles that are often misunderstood by other people. How many people must suffer in silence before we open our eyes to the reality that mental health truly deserves our attention and urgently demands a solution?

The stigma surrounding it is one of the greatest obstacles to mental health awareness. People who are experiencing it are often labeled as helpless, weak, or incapable of handling life’s challenges. However, mental health is a serious problem that needs to be addressed. Such misconceptions affect more than just hurt feelings since they discourage mental health victims from seeking the help and support they urgently need. According to News-Medical’s article, World Mental Health Support and the Effect of Stigma and Discrimination, stigma and discrimination create noise or barriers when it comes to accessing mental health services and also causes delay in the process of seeking proper care. These negative behaviors can also influence or affect a person’s education, employment, and the ability of an individual to participate in society. When people in society are judgmental towards others who are sensitive and vulnerable, it creates a huge problem.

Regardless of the growing awareness of mental health, simply spreading information alone is not enough if society continues to discriminate and treat those who are experiencing mental health struggles differently. Real change begins when people learn to listen without judgment, broaden their knowledge, develop a deeper understanding, and learn to respect others without immediately making assumptions about their situations. We must challenge the beliefs and break down the stereotypes that make people feel humiliated for sharing their own experiences, and stop treating mental health as a weakness that should be concealed and kept hidden. Silence and indifference will only continue to strengthen the stigma, while empathy and understanding can help break it. How can we expect struggling people to speak openly about their hardships in life when society itself makes them afraid of being heard? Mental health should never be a source of shame, and no one should have to defend their personal struggles just to be taken seriously. The greatest change begins when we stop judging the struggles of others and start questioning the stigma that makes them suffer in silence and face difficult battles quietly.

MENTAL HEALTH · EDITORIAL

Mental Health Is Health: The Importance of Caring for Ourselves

ASTRA INK. · EDITORIAL

The consequences of ignoring and neglecting mental health extend beyond individuals who suffer from this problem. Students, in particular, face academic pressures, social isolation, bullying, family problems, and the constant influence of social media that affects their mental well-being. While education often emphasizes academic excellence and measurable achievements, emotional well-being must still be considered and prioritized. A student’s struggles and hardships in life should never be dismissed simply because their grades remain satisfactory. Schools must also acknowledge that academic success isn’t merely about how high the grades are or the number of medals received, because school achievements mean little if students are left without enough emotional support necessary to achieve it.

Furthermore, awareness campaigns alone are not enough to address this problem. We must integrate solutions together to achieve meaningful outcomes. Genuine progress and long-term solutions to resolve this major problem require consistent action. According to the National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH), taking care of one’s mental health involves practicing self-care, maintaining healthy daily habits, and staying connected with people who provide and strengthens emotional support. Therefore, schools must strengthen guidance and counseling support services, communities must create safe social spaces, and the government must be accountable and ensure that accessible mental health services are readily available for those who need them. Above all, families and individuals must learn how to actively listen without immediately judging, making assumptions, or criticizing another person’s experiences.

Supporting mental health does not mean considering every difficult feeling or emotion as a disorder, but rather, understanding that everyone experiences struggles differently and that seeking professional help should never be a source of shame. Mental health must be a huge responsibility for every individual, and raising awareness about this matter is crucial for progress and to prevent an increase in mental health cases. It is time to replace judgment with understanding, empathy, and meaningful support. No one should have to prove that every struggle deserves attention because mental health is health, and taking it seriously should be prioritized and never be optional.

MENTAL HEALTH · FEATURE 01

Beyond Awareness: Building Stronger Mental Health Support for the Youth

ASTRA INK. · FEATURE

Nowadays, mental health has become a topic often discussed in relation to the youth. Whereas, it affects a person’s whole wellbeing including the emotional, physical, psychological, and social factors. Every day, awareness for mental health continues to grow, yet, despite this, access to professional care remains limited. Meaning, just because it is recognized by many doesn’t automatically mean that people receive the help that they need. Awareness will always be important, though it should not only stop because a problem is recognized. Taking action against the problem is equally important, especially with a problem that is an essential part of an individual’s well-being.

Given that this is a growing social issue in our society, rather than convincing people that mental health is real, a solid solution must be ensured that there would be a responsive system that is ready to take action for students, families, and communities in need of help. According to an article published by PhilStar on November 2, 2026 titled “No Child left behind: Unilab Foundation supports stronger collaboration on youth mental health” discusses and addresses the importance of connecting schools, families, healthcare providers, and communities to ensure proper assistance is received. Through a forum organized by the Unilab foundation, it brought together 150 stakeholders from healthcare, academe, government, civil society, and the private sector in order to strengthen the mental health services for children and adolescents. According to Dr. Vanessa-Maria F. Torres-Ticzon, “adolescents increasingly seek independence and acceptance from their peers, while parents remain important sources of guidance and emotional security.” She also urged young people to be able to gain the ability to cope with setbacks instead of shielding them from difficulties. The article also addresses the gaps in mental health services, and due to this, DepEd is deploying 10,000 school counselor associates in order to support guidance services inside the school. Lastly, connection of support systems and stronger coordination among institutions were discussed to further bridge the gaps. “We will continue strengthening connections, sharing responsibilities, and building pathways that make support more accessible, available, responsive, and effective for every learner” as stated by Marie Joan Armada-Urieta, program director of Heads Up Ph. The importance of mental health services should not be underestimated, as early access can help individuals redirect and understand their problems, rather than only realizing that they need help when they are already too deep into drowning.

Given this, providing the youth with early access to mental health services is important because it is sooner to detect and manage their emotions before it becomes difficult and heavier to manage. Guidance must be ensured all throughout, as one is exposed to experiencing setbacks, difficulties, and failures. Furthermore, Dr. Torres-Ticzon’s statement also states that the relationship of an individual with their peers and parents are an essential factor that may affect their mental health and emotional well-being that needs to feel accepted and belonging somewhere. This can be supported by Abraham Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs, that an individual needs social connection and acceptance with the people around them to feel fulfilled. According to the earlier statement, 150 stakeholders were brought together – meaning, this shows that the concern about mental health cannot be fixed by a singular department. It is not a mere responsibility of only the government, but rather a collaborative effort that involves many different institutions and individuals. Bringing together different institutions allows the concern to be seen from multiple perspectives and lenses that help bridge the gap involving mental health.

Ultimately, the concern that circles on mental health calls for effective actions and not only mere recognition. Children and adolescents must receive proper guidance, given the information that every child has their own needs, like what was addressed in Abraham Maslow’s hierarchy of needs. Providing proper support, guidance, and help towards the youth is essential as it will help shape them into who they become. As individuals who understand their emotions, overcome difficulties, and reach their full potential. Through a society that has accessible services and a community that looks out for one another, it can help improve the overall well-being of an individual.

MENTAL HEALTH · FEATURE 02

A Collective Responsibility: Strengthening Mental Health Services

ASTRA INK. · FEATURE

As the rates of suicides rapidly increase this year, 2026, mental health becomes a social issue that can no longer be overlooked. Mental health has been a societal concern for a few years, but as it gets surrounded by the stigma and limited services, judgement and misconceptions for it tends to make louder noise than awareness, leading the efforts for awareness become overshadowed. As issues towards mental health raise awareness, our country still faces numerous challenges that hinder the improvement of one’s mental health – such as, limited services, lack of collaborative responsibility, stigma, and lack of guidance.

Furthermore, a related article that was issued on September 10, 2026 by Jan Escosio titled “Mark Villar seeks stronger mental health services amid rise in suicide cases.” In this article, it recognizes and cites the increase in suicide rates in recent years. In a report issued, Villar cited that according to the Philippine Statistics Authority shows 3,379 deaths from intentional self in 2024, which increased from 3,133 in 2023. In addition to that, in Metro Manila alone, the National Capital Region Police (NCRPO) recorded a 236% increase due to 111 recorded cases from January to March 2026, compared to 33 during the same period last year. Villar pointed out the severe shortage of professionals, “how can we expect Filipinos to seek help when help itself remains difficult to access?” stated by Villar. He also proposed sufficient funding for the comprehensive implementation of existing mental health laws and to improve the country’s mental health care system. He called on families, friends, and communities to check on their loved ones, encouraging them to end the stigma and listen without judgement.

Prevention will always be better than cure, as we always hear. The need for more accessible and stronger mental health services must be extended beyond recognition and awareness. These services should not be underestimated considering there are also factors that hinder the possible effectiveness of these services. We must also consider that the stigma and stereotypes surrounding mental health also influence the perception and actions of the people who experience it. Oftentimes, people have difficulty explaining what they are experiencing or feeling due to the feeling of judgement. Which is why we must realize that we all have a collective responsibility when it comes to mental health. It is not something that the government can manage alone because we all have a role to play even as a student, a daughter or son, and as a citizen. Communities must work together in recognizing and promoting understanding to be a step closer to achieving a society that helps one another.

With all these in mind and in light of these circumstances, the growing concern and worry about mental health, it calls for a more solid and comprehensive approach that will go beyond recognition and awareness. Though, as important it is to realize that awareness is essential in recognizing these problems, the purpose must always go beyond knowledge and understanding that there is a problem. Stronger and more accessible services and collaboration in communities are essential. A person’s well being is not merely affected and to be solved by them alone, but rather a responsibility shared through one’s family, friends, and community because an individual must not be forced to navigate their problems by themselves.

Maraming kabataan ngayon ang may pinagdaraanan na hindi nila kayang ikuwento sa iba. Minsan, hindi sila nagsasalita dahil natatakot silang husgahan o hindi maintindihan ng mga tao sa paligid nila.

Ayon sa isang pag-aaral na tinalakay sa Frontiers in Psychiatry, mahalagang pagtuunan ng pansin ang stigma na nararanasan ng mga kabataang may problema sa mental health. Sa halip na tulungan, may mga pagkakataong minamaliit pa ang kanilang nararamdaman.

Ilang beses na ba nating narinig ang mga katagang “nag-iinarte ka lang” o “isipin mo na lang ang positibo”? Madaling sabihin ang mga ito kung hindi ikaw ang nakararanas ng problema. Hindi natin alam ang pinagdaraanan ng bawat tao, kaya sana ay matuto tayong umunawa bago manghusga.

Hindi masamang aminin na nahihirapan ka. Hindi rin nakakahiya ang humingi ng tulong. Minsan, ang kailangan lang ng isang tao ay may makikinig sa kaniya nang walang halong panghuhusga. Kaya bago tayo magsalita, isipin muna natin kung makatutulong ba ang ating mga salita o lalo lamang makasasakit.

KALUSUGAN · BALITA 01

Krisis sa Mental Health sa Pilipinas, Dapat Nang Tugunan ng Mas Mabilis

ASTRA INK. · BALITA

Kung susuriin ang mga ulat mula sa iba’t ibang health sector, tinatayang aabot sa mahigit 12 milyong Pilipino ang kasalukuyang nakakaranas ng iba’t ibang mental health conditions. Ito ay hindi basta-bastang sumulpot lamang. Malaking bahagi nito ang pressure sa trabaho o eskwela at pati na rin ang tila walang tigil na ingay at impluwensya ng social media sa ating araw-araw na buhay.

Patuloy ang pagbibigat ng pasanin ng maraming Pilipino pagdating sa usaping mental health. Sa gitna ng mga bagong datos mula sa mga eksperto, lumalabas na hindi na biro ang bilang ng mga humaharap ng anxiety at depression ngayon.

Makikita rin ito sa mga lumabas na resulta sa AXA Mind Health Report kamakailan. Ayon dito, mas nagiging mabigat at komplikado raw ang pinagdaraanan ng mga kababayan natin dahil sabay-sabay silang sinusubok ng iba’t ibang klase ng istres. Sa ganitong kalagayan, kapag ganito ang sitwasyon, apektado na ang lahat, mula sa personal na buhay hanggang sa pagganap sa trabaho at paaralan. Ang nakakabahala pa ay dahil sa hirap maghanap ng agarang tulong, napipilitan tuloy ang ilan, lalo na ang mga kabataan na dumiskarte sa AI o Artificial Intelligence para lang may makausap at makapaglabas ng sama ng loob habang naghahanap ng mas ligtas na espasyo para sa kanilang nararamdaman.

Sa kabila ng pagsisikap ng gobyerno na dagdagan ang pondo para sa Mental Health Act, nananatiling malaking hamon ang kakulangan sa mga pasilidad at lisensyadong propesyonal sa bansa. Sa kasalukuyan, nananatiling napakababa ng rasyon ng mga psychiatrist at psychologist kumpara sa pangkalahatang populasyon ng Pilipinas, dahilan upang maging mabagal at mahal ang pagkuha ng propesyonal na tulong para sa karamihan. Ang ganitong sitwasyon ay lalo pang pinahihirap ng umiiral na ring stigma o maling pananaw ng lipunan tungkol sa mga sakit sa pag-iisip.

Binigyang-diin ng mga advocate at organisasyon tulad ng Philippine Mental Health Association (PMHA) at MentalHealthPH na hindi na dapat maghintay ng matinding krisis o burnout bago batin at tulungan ang isang indibidwal. Nanawagan sila sa mga pampubliko at pribadong sektor na magtatag ng mas accessible na community-based mental health programs, palakasin ang mga hotline para sa crisis intervention, at bumuo ng mga kapaligiran sa trabaho at paaralan na nagtataguyod ng bukas na talakayan nang walang paghuhusga.

MENTAL HEALTH · BALITA 02

Mental Health ng mga Kabataan: Pagtutulungan ng Buong Lipunan, Kailangan!

ASTRA INK. · BALITA

Mabigat pa rin ang usapin ng Mental Health sa kabataang Pinoy sa Pilipinas. Dahil dito, nagdesisyon ang Department of Education, o mas kilala sa pinaikli na DepEd, na kumuha ng mahigit kumulang na 10,000 guidance counselor associates para magkaroon ng malalapitan ang mga estudyante at kabataan na patuloy na humaharap sa pagsubok sa kanilang kalusugan, kaisipan, at sa kani-kanilang mga paaralan. Ayon sa ulat ng Philstar nitong November 2, 2026, binigyang-diin na hindi kakayanin ng DepEd lamang ang sinabing krisis. Mas kakailanganin ang sama-samang pagkilos ng buong lipunan upang maiparamdam na hindi sila nag-iisa, para sa mga kabataan na mayroon silang masasandalan at may maipagpapahayagan ng kanilang damdamin sa tuwing may pinagdaraanan na problema o hamon sa buhay.

Nananatiling mabigat ang usapin ng kalusugang pangkaisipan sa Pilipinas dahil marami sa mga kabataan ang mas pinipiling manahimik na lamang. Natatakot silang humingi ng tulong dahil sa pangambang masabihang "nag-iinarte," sabihan ng "hindi na uso 'yan," o kaya ay balewalain lamang ng kanilang sariling pamilya. Sa kawalan ng nakikinig, lalong lumalala ang sitwasyon ng isang indibidwal, kung saan, sa halip na makatulong ang kapwa, ay lalo lamang nitong napapabigat ang suliranin.

Dahil dito, napakalaki ng gampanin ng mga magulang at guro bilang mga unang nakakapansin sa anumang pagbabago sa kilos, gawi, at pang-araw-araw na gawa ng isang bata. Minsan, hindi naman agad kinakailangan ng malaking solusyon; sapat na ang tapat na pakikinig nang walang halong pangaral, pagmamarunong, at lalong-lalo na ang panghuhusga.

Hindi mairesolba ng iisang grupo o pangkat lamang ang malaking usaping ito. Kinakailangan ang mahigpit na pagtutulungan ng pamilya, paaralan, barangay, at mga dalubhasa sa kalusugan, tulad ng mga doktor, upang makabuo ng ligtas na kapaligiran para sa lahat.

Hangga’t hindi ganap na nababasag ang diskriminasyon at negatibong pagtingin sa mental health, marami pa ring kabataan ang patuloy na matatakot humingi ng tulong, lalo na sa kanilang mga mahal sa buhay at mga malalapit na kakilala.
